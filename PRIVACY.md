# Privacy Policy

[中文](#zh)

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

This policy covers Glass, the terminal for macOS, Windows and Linux, and Glass Terminal for iPhone ("Glass").

## We collect nothing

Glass has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. Nothing you type, see or store in Glass is sent to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your device

- The machines you add (name, address, port, user name) and your settings.
- The SSH key Glass makes for you. On iPhone the private key is kept in the Keychain (in the Secure Enclave where available) and never leaves the device; Glass shows you only the public key, to add to your machines.
- On iPhone, a password you type to add Glass's key to a machine is used for that one connection and not stored.
- Host keys of the machines you connect to, so Glass can warn you if one changes.
- On the desktop, passwords you choose to remember and the output Glass restores after a restart, in your own user folder.

## What goes to your own machines

Glass connects only to the machines you add, over SSH, directly from your device. Photos, files or clipboard text you choose to upload or paste are sent to that machine only. Camera and photo access are used only when you pick something to upload.

## Children

Glass is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/devfchen/glass-terminal-releases/issues> or email connect.fengchen@gmail.com.

# 隐私政策

*最后更新：2026 年 11 月 2 日*

本政策适用于 Glass（macOS、Windows、Linux 上的终端）和 iPhone 上的 Glass Terminal（下称 Glass）。

## 我们不收集任何数据

Glass 没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有崩溃上报，也没有自己的服务器。你在 Glass 里输入、看到和保存的内容都不会发给我们或任何其他人。

## 只存在你设备上的内容

- 你添加的机器（名称、地址、端口、用户名）和你的设置。
- Glass 为你生成的 SSH 密钥。在 iPhone 上私钥存放在钥匙串里（设备支持时存放在安全隔区），不会离开设备；Glass 只给你看公钥，供你添加到自己的机器上。
- 在 iPhone 上，为把 Glass 的密钥加到机器上而输入的密码只用于那一次连接，不会保存。
- 你连接过的机器的主机密钥，用于在它发生变化时提醒你。
- 在电脑上：你选择记住的密码，以及 Glass 重启后恢复的输出，都存放在你自己的用户目录里。

## 发送到你自己机器上的内容

Glass 只连接你添加的机器，通过 SSH 从你的设备直接连接。你选择上传或粘贴的照片、文件、剪贴板文字只发送到那台机器。只有在你选择要上传的内容时才会使用相机和照片权限。

## 儿童

Glass 不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

## 变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在这里发布新版本。有问题请在 <https://github.com/devfchen/glass-terminal-releases/issues> 提 issue，或发邮件到 connect.fengchen@gmail.com。
